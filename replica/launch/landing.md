## 2. The Problem: The Quiet Reading Room Has Been Monetized

Reading on a computer was supposed to offer convenience: immediate access to literature, adjustable typography for eye comfort, and instant note-taking for research. Instead, the modern desktop reading landscape has degraded into two extremes:

### The Commercial Upsell Gauntlet
Commercial desktop readers increasingly behave like aggressive adware. Opening a new chapter triggers full-screen upgrade modals. Certain applications even force readers to wait through mandatory 10-second countdown timers before letting them turn the page. What should be a tranquil reading sanctuary becomes an obstacle course of upselling interruptions.

### Artificial Paywalls on Fundamental Tools
Basic reading utilities that should come standard are systematically locked behind paywalls. Users discover their library is capped at ten books unless they buy a license. Custom organizational shelves are disabled. Highlighting passages and exporting study notes to HTML are gated behind expensive "lifetime" licenses that quietly deactivate when the vendor pushes a major version update.

### The Complexity Trap
The most prominent open-source alternative provides format flexibility, but its user interface feels like an intimidating industrial database spreadsheet from twenty years ago. Readers who simply want an elegant bookshelf and comfortable reading typography are caught between intrusive commercial nagware and overwhelming technical bloat.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I migrate my existing library from Calibre or other reader folders?
Yes. Simply drag and drop your existing ebook collection folder directly into FolioHaven. Because FolioHaven uses non-destructive indexing and cryptographic hash deduplication, it catalogs your books without duplicating files or rearranging your folder structure on disk. You can safely point FolioHaven at your existing Calibre library directory.

### How does FolioHaven handle DRM-protected books?
FolioHaven is built for open, private reading and supports DRM-free EPUB, PDF, MOBI, FB2, CBZ, and TXT files. It does not contain decryption keys for proprietary Adobe Adept or Amazon Kindle DRM systems. If an encrypted file is opened, FolioHaven displays a calm, helpful explanation rather than crashing or freezing.

### Does FolioHaven require an account or an internet connection?
Never. FolioHaven is strictly local-first. There is no account sign-up, no email registration, no cloud login, and zero network telemetry. All library records, bookmarks, highlights, and reading statistics remain stored on your local machine in an open SQLite database.

### Is FolioHaven truly free, or will subscription paywalls be added later?
FolioHaven is released under an open-source license. The desktop application is free and will always remain free. We will never add paywalls, library size limits, or nag popups. The project is sustained purely through voluntary patron contributions and GitHub sponsorships from readers who appreciate quiet software.

### What operating systems and platforms are supported?
FolioHaven runs as a native 64-bit application on Windows 10 and Windows 11. It is also available as a modern web application accessible from any standard desktop browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) with local IndexedDB storage.
