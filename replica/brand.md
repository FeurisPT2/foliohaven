## 1. Step 1: The Name Generation & Selection

### The Brand Angle
From the market sentiment research in `fixes.md`, 50% of public reviews attacked the original app's aggressive monetization: intrusive Pro upgrade popups, 10-second countdown reading timers, and artificial paywalls on basic tasks (adding more than 10 books, creating custom categories, exporting notes).

* **Positioning Angle**: **"The Anti-Nag Free Reader"**
* **Core Promise**: *"The gorgeous, modern digital bookshelf and distraction-free reader—completely free, open-source, and forever unrestricted."*

### Cutting to the Top 5 Finalists

We applied the strict trademark and positioning filters:
1. **Zero Confusion**: Not confusingly similar in sound, appearance, or connotation to *Icecream Apps*, *Icecream Ebook Reader*, or category incumbents (*Calibre*, *Kindle*, *Moon+*, *FBReader*, *ReadEra*, *Sumatra*).
2. **Instant Spellability**: Short, phonetically unambiguous after hearing once, free of embarrassing cross-language meanings.
3. **Aligned with the Angle**: Communicates tranquility, literary craftsmanship, and a protected refuge from commercial noise.

The 5 shortlisted finalists:
1. **FolioHaven** (Compound: Folio + Haven)
2. **BookSanctum** (Metaphor: Book + Sanctum)
3. **Folivio** (Invented: Folio + Vivid)
4. **ScribeShelf** (Compound: Scribe + Shelf)
5. **PoreLeaf** (Verb + Noun: Pore + Leaf)

## 5. Step 5: Brand Voice & Product Copy

### The Three Voice Principles

1. **Serene, not sterile**:
   * *What it means*: Warm, tranquil, and literary. Speaks with quiet confidence and warmth.
   * *What it does NOT mean*: Cold, clinical, or detached minimalism.

2. **Capable, not overwhelming**:
   * *What it means*: Powerful tools (multi-format rendering, four-color annotations, HTML study export) presented cleanly when needed.
   * *What it does NOT mean*: Crowding the reading canvas with toolbars, debug stats, or jargon.

3. **Respectful, not apologetic**:
   * *What it means*: Treats the reader as an autonomous book lover who owns their library and data. Never nags, begs, or interrupts.
   * *What it does NOT mean*: Shy or meek when stating formats, DRM constraints, or errors plainly.

---

### Five Do & Don't Pairs

1. **DO** speak like a thoughtful book curator and librarian.  
   **DON'T** speak like a growth-hacked SaaS funnel or adware suite.
2. **DO** use clear, direct action verbs (*"Add Book"*, *"Export Study Notes"*, *"Move to Shelf"*).  
   **DON'T** use artificial urgency, FOMO, or countdown timers (*"Upgrade to Pro now!"*, *"Hurry, only 2 books left in trial"*).
3. **DO** state errors and edge cases with constructive resolution (*"This EPUB uses proprietary DRM encryption. FolioHaven opens DRM-free books to preserve your reading privacy."*).  
   **DON'T** show cryptic crash codes or passive-aggressive dead ends.
4. **DO** celebrate the reader's personal collection and milestones (*"Finished 184 pages"*, *"Your library is calm and up to date"*).  
   **DON'T** lecture or prescribe reading quotas.
5. **DO** keep dialogs quiet, focused, and dismissible with Escape.  
   **DON'T** trigger unprompted popups, promotional banners, or survey modals during a reading session.
